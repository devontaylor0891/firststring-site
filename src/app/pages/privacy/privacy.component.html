The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your team: your name, a way to contact you, your team membership, and your availability for games.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never have.
  • We do not run ads and we do not share your data with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs in the FirstString mobile app. There is no third-party behavioural tracking inside the app.
  • We do not track your device location. The app never requests GPS access. Any location in FirstString is a city or venue that you typed in and chose yourself.
  • You can delete your account and your personal data at any time. See Deleting your account and data.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us

Account information. When you create an account we collect:

  • Your name (first and last, or a full name)
  • Your email address and/or mobile phone number — at least one is required so your team can reach you and so you can recover your account
  • A password, if you sign up with email and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are stored as salted hashes. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Your time zone, so game times display correctly for you
  • A profile photo, if you choose to upload one
  • Optionally, your home city or area, so managers can find local players. This is a place you search for and select — we store the place name, formatted address, and its coordinates. This is not your device's location.

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from that provider so we can create your account. We do not receive your password, your contact list, your friend list, or your posts, and we do not post anything to those accounts.

If you sign in with Apple, we receive only what you agree to share. If you choose "Hide My Email", Apple gives us a private relay address instead of your real one, and we never see your actual email address. Apple sends your name only on your very first sign-in, so if you decline to share it, we will ask you to enter a name yourself.

Team and player information. As you use the Service we store:

  • The teams you belong to, and your role on each (player, manager, assistant manager)
  • Your roster placement (first, second, or third string), your positions, and your status on the roster
  • Your RSVPs, attendance, call-ups, waitlist position, and nudge history for games
  • Join requests you send to public teams, and invitations you send or receive
  • Messages you send in team chat, including automated announcements sent on a manager's behalf

Team and game information created by managers. If you run a team, we store the team name, sport, logo, colours, roster settings, RSVP windows, and the schedule — including game times and venue locations you select.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Push notification tokens. If you enable notifications, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token for each device so we can send you game reminders and call-ups. Tokens are removed when you sign out or disable notifications.
  • Basic technical and security logs. Our infrastructure providers record IP addresses, timestamps, device and browser type, and error diagnostics as part of normal operation. We use these to keep the Service running, to debug problems, and to detect abuse.

The mobile app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no cross-app tracking of any kind. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA or Android Advertising ID) and we do not present App Tracking Transparency prompts because we do not track you.

2.3 What we deliberately do not collect

  • Precise or background device location (GPS)
  • Your date of birth
  • Financial or payment information — the Service is currently free and we do not process payments
  • Health, biometric, racial, religious, political, or other special-category data
  • Your device's full address book (see Contacts and invitations)
  • Photos or files from your device, other than a profile or team image you deliberately choose to upload

3. How we use it

We use your information only to operate and improve the Service. Specifically:

  • To create and maintain your account and let you sign in
  • To show your team its roster, schedule, and who is available for each game
  • To run the features you asked for — RSVPs, automatic call-ups when someone drops out, waitlist promotion, reminders, and team messaging
  • To deliver invitations by email or SMS when you invite someone to a team
  • To send you push notifications you have enabled
  • To respond when you contact us for support
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and meet our legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract for running your account and your team; legitimate interests for security, abuse prevention, and support; consent for push notifications, contact access, and marketing email, each of which you can withdraw at any time; and legal obligation where the law requires us to retain or disclose information.

5. What your teammates can see

This is the part people most often want to know, so it gets its own section.

  • Other members of your team can see your name, profile photo, roster string, position, RSVP status and attendance history, and any messages you post in team chat.
  • Your team manager can additionally see the email address and/or phone number on your account, so they can reach you about games. Managers can also add, remove, and change the roster status of members.
  • If a team is set to public, its name, sport, and general area are discoverable by other FirstString users searching for teams to join. Your individual membership details are not exposed in that search.
  • Your home city, if you set one, may be used to match you with nearby public teams.

Anything you post in team chat is visible to that team and is retained as part of the team's history. Please treat it as you would any group message.

6. Contacts and invitations

The mobile app can help you invite someone by letting you pick a person from your device's contacts, so you do not have to retype their phone number or email.

This works in a deliberately narrow way:

  • The contact picker only opens when you tap to use it.
  • We only ever receive the single contact you select — their name, and one phone number or email address. Your address book is never read in bulk, uploaded, scanned, or stored.
  • The details you select are used to prefill the invitation form. You can edit them before sending.
  • Once sent, we store the invited person's name and contact detail as part of the pending invitation so we can deliver it and so the manager can see who has been invited. The invitation record is deleted when it is accepted, declined, revoked, or expires.
  • You can decline the contacts permission and still invite people by typing their details in manually. Nothing else in the app depends on it.

If you are invited to a team by someone else, we received your name and contact detail from the person who invited you. If you would rather not be in our records, tell us at {{ contactEmail }} and we will delete the invitation.

7. Push notifications, email and SMS

Push notifications are optional and are used for game reminders, call-ups when a spot opens, roster changes, and team messages. You can turn them off in your device settings at any time.

Email and SMS are used for team invitations and essential account messages such as password resets. Standard message and data rates may apply to text messages. Invitation texts are one-off and transactional — we do not run SMS marketing campaigns.

Marketing email is only sent to people who joined our early-access list on the website. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link.

8. The website

The marketing site at firststringapp.com does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any behavioural tracking. We do not set our own tracking cookies and there is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Two things on the site are worth naming:

  • The early-access signup form is hosted by Brevo and embedded in the page. When you submit it, your email address goes to Brevo, and Brevo may set its own cookies in that embedded frame. Brevo's privacy policy governs that processing.
  • The "Share on Facebook" buttons are plain links. They open a Facebook share window only when you click one. No Facebook tracking pixel runs on our site.

Our web host keeps standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting.

If you contact us — through the contact form, by email, or in an app support request — we keep your message and contact details for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of the issue.

9. How long we keep things

  • Account and profile data is kept while your account is active, and is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Push notification tokens are deleted when you sign out, disable notifications, or delete your account.
  • Pending invitations are deleted once accepted, declined, revoked, or expired.
  • Team, game, and attendance records belong to the team. If you leave or delete your account, your personal profile is removed, but the team's historical record of a game may retain an anonymised entry so past results and counts stay intact.
  • Team chat messages remain visible to the team as part of its history, in the same way an email you sent remains in the recipient's inbox. If you need specific messages removed, contact us.
  • Marketing list subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Server and security logs are retained on a short rolling basis by our infrastructure providers, typically no more than 30 to 60 days.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your FirstString account and the personal information attached to it at any time, and you do not need to have the app installed to do so.

In the app: go to Profile → Edit → Delete Account. You will be shown exactly what will be removed, asked to confirm your sign-in, and asked to type DELETE. Deletion then happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app: email {{ contactEmail }} from the email address on your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request". If your account uses a phone number rather than an email address, include that number so we can verify it is yours.

Either way, here is what happens:

  • We verify the request is genuinely from you.
  • We delete your account credentials, name, email address, phone number, profile photo, time zone, home location, push notification tokens, team memberships, RSVPs, and pending invitations.
  • Messages you posted in team chat and historical game records may be retained by the team in anonymised form, so the team's own history is not corrupted. Tell us if you also want your past messages removed and we will remove them.
  • We may retain a minimal record where the law requires it, or where we need it to resolve a dispute or enforce our terms.
  • We complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email when it is done.

If you manage a team, deleting your account deletes that team along with its roster, schedule, messages and invitations. The app names the affected teams before you confirm. If you would rather hand a team to someone else, contact us before deleting and we will transfer it.

12. Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure and is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to the database is governed by server-enforced security rules, so one team cannot read another team's roster or messages. Passwords are hashed by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us. API credentials for our email and SMS providers are held in managed secret storage.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

13. Where your data lives

FirstString is operated from Canada, and our providers — Google, Resend, Twilio, and Brevo — store and process data on servers in Canada, the United States, and elsewhere. This means your information may be transferred outside your home country, and may be accessible to courts and law enforcement in those countries under their local laws.

Where we transfer personal data out of the UK or EEA, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent safeguard offered by the provider.

14. Age requirement

FirstString is intended for people aged 16 and over. The Service is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

If a manager needs to track a player under 16 on a roster, that player should not create an account. The manager remains responsible for any information they enter about that player, and for having the right to enter it.

If you believe someone under 16 has created an account, contact {{ contactEmail }} and we will delete the account and its data promptly.
